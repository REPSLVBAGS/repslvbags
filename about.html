Our Story

About Reps LV Bags

Quiet luxury, honestly made.

01 What We Believe

A great handbag shouldn't need a logo to be recognised. We started Reps LV Bags because we believed women deserved beautiful, well-made bags at prices that didn't require a sacrifice. Every piece in our collection is chosen for its silhouette, its colour story and the way it carries you through real life — work, weekends, dinners and everything between.

02 How We Select

We don't chase trends. Each bag is evaluated for proportion, material quality, hardware finish and everyday practicality before it earns a place in the collection. If a piece doesn't feel right in the hand, it doesn't make the cut.

03 Our Commitment

  • Transparent pricing with no hidden fees
  • Personal order verification before dispatch
  • Live order tracking from checkout to doorstep
  • 30-day returns on unworn items
  • Real humans answering supportrepslvbags@gmail.com within 24 hours
