Shipping Policy

Everything you need to know about how we ship your order.

1 Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1–2 business days after your Cash App payment is confirmed. Orders placed on weekends are processed on Monday.

2 Shipping Options

  • Standard Shipping (3–5 business days) — FREE on orders over $150, otherwise $9.99
  • Express Shipping (1–2 business days) — $19.99

3 Carriers

We ship via USPS, UPS, and FedEx. You will receive a tracking number by email once your order ships.

4 Delivery

We currently ship to all 50 US states. Delivery times are estimates and may vary during holidays.

5 Order Tracking

Track your order anytime at repslvbags.com/track-order using your order number and email.

6 Wrong Address

Please double-check your address at checkout. We are not responsible for orders shipped to an incorrect address provided by the customer.

7 Contact

If your package is delayed over 7 days, email us at supportrepslvbags@gmail.com

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days after processing (1–2 business days). Express takes 1–2 business days. Total time from order to door is typically 4–7 business days.

How do I return?

Email supportrepslvbags@gmail.com with your order number and reason within 30 days of delivery. We'll send a return label within 24 hours.

When will I get my refund?

Once we receive and inspect your return (2–3 business days), your refund is sent via Cash App within 3–5 business days.
